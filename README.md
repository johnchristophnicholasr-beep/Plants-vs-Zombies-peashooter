# Plants vs Zombies Peashooter

A 2D, English-only, no-ads Android prototype inspired by the classic lane-defense style.

## Gameplay
- One playable plant at a time.
- Six lanes.
- Day/Night/Roof use Peashooter.
- Pool/Night Pool use Threepeater.
- Auto-shooting; move the plant between lanes.
- Random zombie lane spawning.
- Five worlds × 10 waves.
- Wave 10 is the world boss.
- Final Zomboss battle.
- Tutorial 1-1 and 1-2.
- Random buffs: Fire Pea, Snow Pea, Obsidian Pea, damage, HP, defense, Random Pick.
- Roof/final boss minigun variants.

Artwork in this starter is original placeholder artwork, not copied franchise assets.
